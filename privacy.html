Universal TV Remote - Remotify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Universal TV Remote - Remotify ("Remotify", "the app") handles and why. Remotify is developed by Mehmet Emin Yazıcıoğlu, an individual developer ("we", "us").

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • The app talks directly to your TV on your home network. Nothing about your TVs, what you do with them or your photos is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. To understand how subscriptions perform, the app sends subscription and basic usage analytics to Adapty, a subscription analytics service. These are not linked to your name or Apple Account.

Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your saved TVs: the name the TV reports, the name you give it, its brand, model, network address and hardware (MAC) address.
  • Pairing keys that Samsung and LG TVs issue after you press Allow, and the certificate the app creates to pair with Android TV and Google TV. These are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your Quick Launch apps and settings, such as appearance, haptics and onboarding progress.

Removing a TV in the app deletes its details and pairing key. Deleting the app removes the rest; iOS may keep Keychain pairing keys after an app is deleted, and they are only used again if you reinstall Remotify. This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which Apple and you control.

Information shared with your TV

To control your TV, the app sends it button presses, the text you type in the keyboard, and the app you choose to open. It reads the list of apps installed on the TV to show them in the app. This happens only on your local network, between your iPhone and your TV.

Photos and videos

Remotify doesn't ask for access to your photo library. When you use Cast, you choose photos and videos in Apple's photo picker, and the app receives only the items you choose. It prepares a copy in a format TVs can play and serves it to your TV over your local network while you cast. The copies are kept in the app's temporary storage, are deleted when you choose different items, and are never uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find TVs on the Wi-Fi you're connected to and to control them. The app only connects to devices on that local network.

You can change this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Subscriptions

Payments, renewals and refunds are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, e-mail, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks whether your subscription is active using Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone.

Subscription analytics (Adapty)

The app uses the Adapty SDK, provided by Adapty Tech Inc., to measure subscriptions: how many people start a trial or subscription, renew or cancel, and in which countries. Adapty only observes purchases; it doesn't handle payments. It receives:

  • A random identifier created for this installation of the app, and Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV). The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected.
  • Subscription purchases, renewals and cancellations: the product, price, currency, dates and Apple's transaction identifiers.
  • Basic usage events, such as when the app is opened.
  • Device and app details: device model, iOS version, app version, language, App Store country and IP address, which is used to estimate your country. If you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads ad, the attribution token Apple provides for that install.

This information is not linked to your name, e-mail or Apple Account, and it is not used to track you or to show ads. Adapty processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy and keeps it for as long as needed to provide these statistics. It never includes anything about your TVs, what you do with them or your photos.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

Remotify is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

The only information about you held outside your iPhone is the subscription analytics described above, which is not linked to your identity. If you want it deleted, contact us and we'll ask Adapty to delete the data for your installation. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you have rights over personal data; contact us at the address below with any question. Data on your iPhone can be deleted by you at any time, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
