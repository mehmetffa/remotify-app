Universal TV Remote - Remotify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Universal TV Remote - Remotify ("Remotify", "the app") handles and why. Remotify is developed by Mehmet Emin Yazıcıoğlu, an individual developer ("we", "us").

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The app talks directly to your TV on your home network. Nothing about your TVs, your use of the app or your photos is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. The app uses no third-party services.

Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your saved TVs: the name the TV reports, the name you give it, its brand, model, network address and hardware (MAC) address.
  • Pairing keys that Samsung and LG TVs issue after you press Allow, and the certificate the app creates to pair with Android TV and Google TV. These are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your Quick Launch apps and settings, such as appearance, haptics and onboarding progress.

Removing a TV in the app deletes its details and pairing key. Deleting the app removes the rest; iOS may keep Keychain pairing keys after an app is deleted, and they are only used again if you reinstall Remotify. This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which Apple and you control.

Information shared with your TV

To control your TV, the app sends it button presses, the text you type in the keyboard, and the app you choose to open. It reads the list of apps installed on the TV to show them in the app. This happens only on your local network, between your iPhone and your TV.

Photos and videos

Remotify doesn't ask for access to your photo library. When you use Cast, you choose photos and videos in Apple's photo picker, and the app receives only the items you choose. It prepares a copy in a format TVs can play and serves it to your TV over your local network while you cast. The copies are kept in the app's temporary storage, are deleted when you choose different items, and are never uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find TVs on the Wi-Fi you're connected to and to control them. The app only connects to devices on that local network.

You can change this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Subscriptions

Payments, renewals and refunds are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, e-mail, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks whether your subscription is active using Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone; this information is not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or subscription SDKs.

Children

Remotify is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't receive or hold information about you, there is no personal data on our side to access or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you have rights over personal data; contact us at the address below with any question. Data on your iPhone can be deleted by you at any time, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
