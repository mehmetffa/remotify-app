Support

Remotify turns your iPhone into a remote for your smart TV over your home Wi-Fi. If you need help, the answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? E-mail us at scoutapp.support@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Connecting to your TV

Which TVs does Remotify work with?

Roku streaming players and Roku TVs, Samsung smart TVs (Tizen, 2016 and later) and LG smart TVs (webOS). The TV has to be a smart TV connected to your network. iPhone has no infrared, so older TVs without Wi-Fi or Ethernet can't be controlled.

The app can't find my TV.

Turn the TV on and make sure your iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, not a guest network. Turn off any VPN on your iPhone. Check that Local Network access is on: iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Remotify. Then tap Scan again. You can also enter the TV's IP address under "Can't find your TV?"; the app shows where to find it on each TV.

Roku: the app connects but buttons don't work.

On the Roku, open Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Default.

Samsung or LG: what is the prompt on my TV?

The first time you connect, the TV asks whether to allow Remotify. Choose Allow with your TV's own remote. The TV remembers this, so you won't be asked again. If you chose Deny, connect again and the prompt reappears.

Using the app

Typing with the keyboard doesn't show anything on the TV.

Open a search or text field on the TV first, then type on your iPhone. The text appears in the field that's selected on the TV.

An app shows "This app isn't available on your TV".

The app isn't installed on your TV, or your TV model uses a different version of it. Install it from your TV's app store and try again.

How do I change the Quick Launch apps?

Touch and hold one of the three apps under the remote and choose another one. You can also touch and hold an app in the Channels tab and choose "Add to Quick launch".

Casting a photo or video doesn't start.

Keep Remotify open while casting, because the TV plays the file directly from your iPhone. Long videos take a few seconds to prepare. Some older TVs can't play every format; if one item fails, try another.

How do I rename or remove a TV?

Tap the TV name at the top of the Remote tab, then swipe left on a TV, or touch and hold it, and choose Rename or Remove.

Subscriptions

What's free and what's in Pro?

The basic remote is free: power, volume, channels, navigation and playback. Remotify Pro adds the keyboard, one-tap app launch, Quick Launch, the touchpad, casting photos and videos, and control of multiple TVs.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Remotify → Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Remotify, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid being charged again. You keep Pro until the end of the period.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.
